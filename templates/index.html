About the Model

This framework uses VGG16 — a 16-layer deep convolutional neural network pre-trained on 1.2 million ImageNet images — adapted for chest X-ray analysis through transfer learning. Training was done in two phases: first warming up a new classification head while keeping the backbone frozen, then fine-tuning the final convolutional block (block5) on X-ray data at a reduced learning rate.

The output is a probability score between 0 and 1. Scores below 0.5 are classified as Normal. Scores at or above 0.5 indicate disease presence. A Grad-CAM heatmap highlights the lung regions that most influenced the prediction.
